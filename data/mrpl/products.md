## 4. Marketing network

**HiQ retail outlets**

| Date | Operational outlets | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Mar-22 | 32 | [src: AR22] |
| Mar-24 | 101 | [src: AR24] |
| Mar-25 | 167 (entered Tamil Nadu) | [src: AR25] |
| Mar-26 | 252 (entered Andhra Pradesh; states: KA, KL, TN, AP) | [src: AR26] |

- **Retail sales:** 153,980 KL in FY24, 229.8 million L in FY25 and 343.9 million L of MS+HSD in FY26. The FY26 report also counts 76 EV chargers at HiQ outlets. [src: AR26]
- **Terminals and depots:**
  - Devangonthi (Bengaluru) terminal: 81 TKL, fed by the PMHBL pipeline, commissioned Q2 FY25. [src: AR25]
  - Record 4.07 MMT of auto fuels and ATF dispatched through PMHBL in FY26. [src: AR26]
  - Depots at Hosur, Hindupur and Kasaragod; third-party terminals at Mangalore and Ennore. [src: AR26]
- **Aviation:** Shell MRPL Aviation Fuels & Services Ltd (SMAFSL) is a 50:50 JV with Shell Gas BV.
  - It serves Bengaluru, Goa, Mangalore, Hyderabad and smaller airports.
  - FY25 volumes: 446,529 KL as delivery company at Indian airports, and 579,980 KL as contracting company for Indian airlines abroad. [src: https://www.icra.in/Rating/GetRationalReportFilePdf?id=137363]
  - Turnover: ₹2,087 Cr in FY24, ₹2,549 Cr in FY25 and ₹2,706 Cr in FY26. [src: AR26]
- **Bulk customers:** OMCs, Indian Railways, state undertakings, PSUs, road contractors, mining and cement companies. [src: AR25]
- **Direct/institutional sales:** 1.99 MMT in FY22, 2.3 MMT in FY23, 2.6 MMT in FY24 and 2.99 MMT in FY25 (₹15,214 Cr). [src: AR25]
- **Polypropylene:** sold through DCA/CS and distributor channels, with warehousing added in Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh in FY26. [src: AR26]
- **Bunkering at NMPA:** no MRPL retail bunkering service could be verified. MRPL exports MFO cargoes, and a new fuel-oil pipeline to NMPA was approved. [src: AR24]
