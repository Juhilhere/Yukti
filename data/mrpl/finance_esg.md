## 8. Caveats & open items

- Revenue: revenue_cr = revenue from operations incl. excise (press releases); sales_net_excise_cr = FY26 AR ten-year table (net of excise).
- FY2016-17..FY2019-20 standalone; FY2020-21 onward 'integrated' (post-OMPL merger, restated) per FY26 AR ten-year table.
- EBITDA basis differs by year (Screener operating profit excl. other income for FY17-FY23; rating-agency PBILDT FY24-25; company-reported FY26).
- Quarterly GRMs for Q2/Q3 FY26 not found in public sources; Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 GRMs from a single secondary source (low confidence).
- FY2021-22 GRM: 8.73 (FY22 AR) vs 8.60 (FY26 AR ten-year table); FY2020-21 GRM 3.71 (FY21 AR, standalone) vs 3.90 (restated integrated). [src: https://admin.mrpl.co.in/img/UploadedFiles/AnnualReport/Files/e48fd029cb5849df9136c87a8ff639d1.pdf] [src: https://admin.mrpl.co.in/img/UploadedFiles/AnnualReport/Files/086a690e6e6248c8a437003311416d01.pdf]
- Throughput basis differs (gross crude vs total input incl. other feed); e.g. FY25 18.044 MMT crude vs 18.18 MMT total; FY26 16.774 crude vs ~17.00 total.
- FY24 SEC: FY25 press release cites FY24 = 71.2 MBN while FY24 BRSR table (poorly extracted) shows 72.94; treat FY24 MBN as uncertain.
- Q1 FY27 YoY revenue growth: correct figure is ~98% (41,609 vs 20,989); some outlets misreport 46%.
- Q4 FY26 PAT (Rs 119 cr) far below PBT (Rs 1,235 cr) due to a large tax charge; the precise reason (e.g. deferred-tax remeasurement) was not confirmed in the sources reviewed.
- Refinery capacity is ~300,000 bpd (15 MMTPA); an OilPrice/Reuters rewrite citing 500,000 bpd is incorrect.
- No 2025-26 India Ratings rating was found; CARE/CRISIL 2026 surveillance updates were not found (latest found: June 2025).
- HPCL-MRPL merger: long-discussed (since 2018) but no 2025-26 step found; ONGC direct 71.63% and HPCL 16.96% as of Mar-2025.
