## 2. Timeline
- **1988** - Incorporated on 7 March 1988 as a JV of HPCL and Indian Rayon & Industries Ltd (A.V. Birla Group) [src: https://mrpl.co.in/en/Content/History]
- **1996** - Phase-I refinery (3.69 MMTPA) commissioned; commercial operations begin (March 1996) [src: https://mrpl.co.in/en/Content/History]
- **1999** - Phase-II completed (Sept 1999), capacity raised to 9.69 MMTPA [src: https://www.offshore-technology.com/projects/mangalorerefineryexp/]
- **2003** - 28 March 2003: ONGC acquires A.V. Birla Group stake and infuses Rs 600 crore; MRPL becomes ONGC subsidiary [src: https://mrpl.co.in/en/Content/History]
- **2006** - ONGC Mangalore Petrochemicals Ltd (OMPL) incorporated (19 Dec 2006) to build aromatic complex in MSEZ [src: https://ongcindia.com/web/eng/about-ongc/jvs/ompl]
- **2007** - Granted Miniratna status by Government of India [src: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangalore_Refinery_and_Petrochemicals_Limited]
- **2009** - Foundation stone for Phase-III refinery expansion-cum-upgradation (Nov 2009) [src: https://www.offshore-technology.com/projects/mangalorerefineryexp/]
- **2012** - CDU/VDU-III commissioned 29 March 2012; Phase-III raises capacity to 15 MMTPA (from 11.82) [src: https://mrpl.co.in/node/225]
- **2014** - OMPL Aromatic Complex (PX ~0.9 MMTPA, benzene ~0.28 MMTPA) commissioned Oct 2014 [src: https://ongcindia.com/web/eng/about-ongc/jvs/ompl]
- **2015** - Feb 2015: MRPL raises stake in OMPL from 3% to 46% [src: https://www.ogj.com/general-interest/companies/article/17246983/mrpl-increases-ownership-in-aromatics-complex]
- **2015** - Polypropylene unit (440 KTPA, MANGPOL) starts commercial production June 2015 - last Phase-III unit [src: https://mrpl.co.in/en/Content/Petrochemical]
- **2018** - ONGC acquires HPCL; HPCL-MRPL merger mooted (board approvals/plans reported Aug 2018) [src: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangalore_Refinery_and_Petrochemicals_Limited]
- **2018** - 27 Feb 2018: COCO retail outlet inaugurated at Panambur, Mangaluru (early HiQ retail network) [src: https://www.mrpl.co.in/new_retail%20_outlet]
- **2020** - BS-VI MS/HSD supply begins (geared from March 2020, ahead of 1 April 2020 national deadline) [src: https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/mrpl-to-supply-bs-vi-grade-fuel-from-march-2020-766720.html]
- **2021** - 1 Jan 2021: MRPL acquires ONGC's ~49% stake in OMPL (Rs 1,216.7 cr), taking holding to 99.9998% [src: https://psuwatch.com/amp/mrpl-acquires-100-percent-shares-in-ompl-from-ongc]
- **2022** - OMPL amalgamated into MRPL: MCA order 14 Apr 2022, appointed date 1 Apr 2021, effective 1 May 2022 [src: https://mrpl.co.in/merge-ompl]
- **2024** - 28 Feb 2024: Mundkur Shyamprasad Kamath becomes MD; 8 Aug 2024: Nandakumar V. Pillai becomes Director (Refinery) [src: https://mrpl.co.in/en/Content/RTI]
- **2025** - 25 Mar 2025: Devendra Kumar takes charge as Director (Finance) & CFO [src: https://www.psuconnect.in/new-faces-in-psus/devendra-kumar-takes-charge-as-director-finance-and-cfo-at-mrpl-board]
- **2025** - FY2025-26: record 16.774 MMT crude (111.8% utilisation), 13 VLCCs handled, 85 new HiQ outlets (total 252), first outlet in Andhra Pradesh, Devangonthi terminal fully operational [src: https://admin.mrpl.co.in/img/UploadedFiles/AnnualReport/Files/a4a35c19a5b64944b130274489f73963.pdf]
- **2025** - Iso-Butyl Benzene (IBB) 200 TPA demo plant mechanically complete Dec 2025 (in-house patented process with CSIR-NCL) [src: https://admin.mrpl.co.in/img/UploadedFiles/AnnualReport/Files/a4a35c19a5b64944b130274489f73963.pdf]
- **2026** - 28 Mar 2026: four Independent Directors complete tenure; Jun-Jul 2026 Dr Seema (Govt nominee) and Satyan Kumar (ONGC nominee) join board; 12 Aug 2026 new CVO [src: https://mrpl.co.in/en/BoardofDirector]
- **2026** - Projects in execution: Power System Upgradation (grid connectivity, Rs 385 cr, Sep 2026), demo Bio-ATF/SAF plant (Jan 2027), 500 TPA green hydrogen (Mar 2027) [src: https://mrpl.co.in/en/Content/Projects]

## 4. Organisation structure & departments
The organogram (as on 20.05.2025) has the MD at the top. Reporting to the MD are Director (Refinery), Director (Finance), ED (Projects), GGM (HR), CGM (Legal), the Company Secretary and CGM (Internal Audit). The CVO reports independently.
- **Director (Refinery):** GGM Materials, and ED (Refinery). ED (Refinery) oversees GGM HSE, Technical Services, Maintenance and Operations, plus CGM (IS).
- **ED (Projects):** GGM Corporate Strategy, Projects, Marketing and Marketing Projects.
- **Director (Finance):** GGM Finance-1, Finance-2 and Impex & Shipping.
[src: https://mrpl.co.in/en/Organization_structure]

## 8. CSR summary
- **Programme:** CSR runs under the name "Samrakshan". Focus areas are education, healthcare, sanitation, environmental sustainability, and empowerment of women and weaker sections. Most work is in Dakshina Kannada.
- **FY2025-26 budget and spend:** the obligation was Rs 65.42 crore and Rs 48.67 crore was spent. The unspent amount for ongoing projects went to the Unspent CSR Account.
- **Major FY2025-26 projects:** classrooms and toilets, anganwadis, CB-NAAT TB machines, PHC equipment, a palliative care centre and mobile cancer screening unit, the Pilikula astronomy gallery, and an artificial limb camp.
