### Install in three steps

1. Double-click **Yukti-Setup.exe**. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click **More info → Run anyway**.
2. Yukti opens and sets itself up: it downloads its AI once. Keep the window open. If the internet drops, it continues by itself.
3. Sign in. From now on Yukti works **without internet**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | 10 or 11, 64-bit |
| **Memory** | 8 GB (16 GB recommended) |
| **Free disk space** | about 10 GB |
| **Graphics card** | not required. An NVIDIA card makes answers faster |
| **First download** | 3.9 GB, or 5.3 GB with an NVIDIA card (15–45 minutes), photos and voice included |

### On a Mac

1. Open **Yukti-macOS-arm64.dmg** and drag **Yukti** into **Applications**.
2. Open Yukti. The first time, macOS says it cannot check the app: open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
3. Yukti downloads its AI model, photos and voice once (about 3.9 GB). Then sign in. From now on Yukti works **without internet**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Mac** | Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 13.3 or later |
| **Memory** | 8 GB (16 GB recommended) |
| **Free disk space** | about 6 GB |

Problems? In Yukti: **Help → Report a problem**, or email **juhilprogramming@gmail.com**.

**Since 0.5.0**

- 📷 **Ask with a photo.** Photograph a pipe label, tag plate or nameplate. Yukti reads it and tells you what the line or equipment is from the plant's documents. It never guesses from the colour, and it points out rust and leaks.
